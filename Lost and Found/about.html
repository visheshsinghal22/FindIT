COLLEGE LOST & FOUND

About FindIT

FindIT is a simple Lost and Found Management System designed for college students.

🎯 Our Purpose

FindIT helps students report lost items and share information about items they have found on campus.

🔎 What We Do

Students can view lost and found items, report an item, and check the available information about belongings.

🤝 Our Goal

Our goal is to make the process of finding and returning lost belongings easier for the college community.
